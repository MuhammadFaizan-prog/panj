Design thinking

Basant is spring in Lahore: kites over the old-city rooftops. The page above is our own AI-generated reference, rebuilt: the backdrop is cut from it and the poster is its photo, while the card is the live player. Its frame, sky-blue bar, white discs, clock and yellow play ring are CSS; the charkhi and the two kites are cut from the reference so they match it exactly. The seek bar is the manja: it climbs from the spool's tip to the orange kite, whose bridle is the playhead. Volume is the blue kite on a short white string, its height the level, inline so it can be reached with Tab and dragged on a touch screen. Every control keeps a 44px target around a smaller drawing, and the sway stops when you ask your system for reduced motion.

  • Seeka tilted string from the charkhi; the kite rides it as the thumb
  • Volumean inline vertical slider, Tab and arrow keys, M to mute
  • Targets44px everywhere, navy ring + white halo on focus
  • Livetime, speed (“1x”), captions (“CC”), fullscreen are stock Video.js v10
