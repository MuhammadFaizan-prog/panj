Basant

Lahore's kite festival, on a rooftop at dusk. A Video.js v10 skin.

Design thinking

Basant is spring in Lahore: kites over the old-city rooftops and yellow everywhere, so the control bar is a strip of daytime sky with a marigold edge. The seek bar is the manja: it unwinds from a wooden charkhi on the left, the wound thread on the spool gets thinner as the video plays, and the playhead is a small kite that sways while playing and tilts as you drag it. Volume is a second kite climbing a slanted string, so its height is the level, and it sits in the bar instead of a hover popover so it can be reached with Tab and dragged on a touch screen. Everything you need to read is navy ink on sky or paper, the kite thumbs keep 44px hit areas around smaller drawings, and all of the sway, unwinding and drifting stops when you ask your system for reduced motion.
