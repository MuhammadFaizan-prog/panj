Design thinking

Ink & Reed treats the player as a page on a calligrapher’s desk rather than a layer over the picture: the controls sit on cream paper below the video, so their contrast never depends on how bright the frame is. The page above is our own AI-generated reference, rebuilt: the sheet behind is the reference itself and the card is the live player, placed on it pixel for pixel. Its paper, gold rule, timeline rule, serif clock and speed are CSS; the gold-leaf band, the reed pen and the hand-drawn glyphs are cut from the reference so they match it exactly. The timeline is a reed pen (qalam): its tapered body is the played stroke and grows as the episode is written, and the nib with its ink drop is the playhead. The ±15 s skips, the “CC” captions bubble and “1×” are stock Video.js v10 controls with 44px targets around smaller drawings. Under a right-to-left layout the bar mirrors and the pen writes from right to left, as Urdu is, with pointer and arrow-key input mirrored to match.
