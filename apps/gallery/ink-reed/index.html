Ink & Reed

Urdu calligraphy podcast player

Mirrors the bar and writes the timeline from right to left; pointer and arrow keys follow.

Design thinking

Ink & Reed treats the player as a page on a calligrapher’s desk rather than a layer over the picture: the controls sit on cream paper below the video, so their contrast never depends on how bright the frame is. The timeline is a reed-pen (qalam) stroke that starts as a hairline and swells towards the end, and the playhead is the nib with an ink drop gathering under its tip. The ±15 s skips, serif time numerals and thin gold-leaf edge come from the same desk, and every piece of art is hand-written SVG and CSS. Under a right-to-left layout the bar mirrors and the stroke is written from right to left, as Urdu is, with pointer and arrow-key input mirrored to match. The only motion is the drop gathering while the episode plays, and it stops when reduced motion is requested.
