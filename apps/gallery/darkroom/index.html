Darkroom

Film lab. Seek on a strip of film, read the time off an LED, under the safelight.

Design thinking

A darkroom is where an image appears slowly, so the controls borrow from the tools you touch there. The seek bar is a strip of 35 mm film: played frames glow as if developed under the safelight, one loupe bracket marks where you are, and hovering pulls a real frame from the video's storyboard. Time reads out on a seven-segment LED drawn as an original font, and the push buttons carry small LEDs that light for playing, muted, captions and fullscreen. Safelight red sets the mood but never carries essential text: digits and labels are bright amber or warm white on a matte black deck, so they read the same over bright and dark footage. Volume is an inline fader you can Tab to and drag on touch, instead of a hover-only popover.
