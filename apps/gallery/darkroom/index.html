Darkroom

Film-lab player

Design thinking

A darkroom is where an image appears slowly, so the player is a piece of lab hardware: a monitor in a safelight-red bezel, a strip of 35 mm film under it and a matte deck. The page above is our own AI-generated reference, rebuilt: the wall is the reference itself and the unit is the live player, placed on it pixel for pixel. The film is a seek control: it runs through the gate as the video plays, so the red loupe always rings the current frame; drag it to scrub, click a frame to jump to it, or Tab to it and use the arrows. The thin line in the picture seeks too. Time reads off a seven-segment LED drawn as an original font; the knob is the volume (drag it up or down, or use the arrows) and its cap mutes; the push keys carry LEDs that light for captions, the current speed and fullscreen, and the play key lights the state it is in. Every key is a 44px target, and the deck is opaque, so its contrast never depends on the footage.
