Design thinking

Glass treats the controls as a lens resting on the picture rather than a bar bolted underneath it. The page above is our own AI-generated reference, rebuilt: the frame and backdrop are cut from it, the poster is its wave, and the pill is real CSS, a backdrop blur with a pale sea-glass tint and a 2px rim, so it frosts whatever footage plays behind it. A thin luminous ring holds the play glyph, the seek fill glows, and every glyph carries a hairline dark halo, so white strokes still hold over foam. Where blur is not supported the pill becomes solid tinted glass, and under reduced transparency or higher contrast it turns near-opaque.

  • Surfacebackdrop blur + brightness, sea-glass tint, 2px rim
  • Fallbackssolid tint without blur; near-opaque for reduced transparency
  • Targets44px everywhere, white ring + dark halo on focus
  • Livetime, speed (“1x”), captions, volume and fullscreen are stock Video.js v10
