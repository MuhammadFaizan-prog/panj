Glass

A frosted control pill that floats over the picture and stays readable on snow or at night.

Footage

Film: the public Video.js demo clip, mostly mid-tones.

Design thinking

Glass treats the controls as a lens resting on the picture rather than a bar bolted underneath it. The pill uses real backdrop blur that also dims what sits behind it, a 1px gradient rim for the specular edge, and a soft gradient scrim beneath, so white glyphs hold up over a white frame. Where blur is not supported the same pill becomes solid tinted glass, and under reduced transparency or higher contrast it turns near-opaque with a visible edge. Motion is a small spring on hover and press, and it switches off under reduced motion. The icons are hand-drawn SVG on a 24px grid; every behaviour underneath is stock Video.js v10.

  • Surfacebackdrop blur + saturate + dim, 1px specular rim
  • Fallbackssolid tint without blur; near-opaque for reduced transparency
  • Targets44px everywhere, white ring + dark halo on focus
  • VolumeTab reaches the slider; on touch, first tap on the speaker opens it
