PHATTA

Phatta: a Pakistani truck-art skin for Video.js

DEKH MAGAR PYAR SE

Design thinking

Phatta borrows from the painted backs of Pakistani goods trucks, where every surface is decorated yet the road still has to read the plate; so the loud art lives on the page and the frame, while the controls stay a calm black lacquer strip with cream icons that read over bright and dark footage.

The seek bar is chamak-patti, the reflective foil tape truck artists cut into patterns: gold beads for what you have watched, silver for what is left, and a polished brass stud for the playhead.

The crown carries a line often painted on truck backs, “Dekh magar pyar se” (دیکھ مگر پیار سے): look, but with love.

Every motif is hand-written SVG and CSS; the bells only sway when you hover the truck and stay still if you prefer reduced motion, and every control keeps a 44 px target and a visible focus ring.
